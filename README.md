Lock In is a pomodoro timer that yells at you. It pops up while you're working and asks if you're actually working, and if you say no (or just ignore it), it gets mad. The whole page slowly turns from calm green to a shaking dark red, and the messages go from "You've got this!" to "LOCK. IN. NOW. YOUR FUTURE SELF IS WATCHING. 😡"

Drill mode after getting caught slacking three times

I'm really bad at focusing. Normal pomodoro timers never worked for me because they just beep and I dismiss them without thinking. What actually works is when a friend sits next to me and goes "are you working or not?" every few minutes, so I decided to build that friend. 

You type in what you're working on, pick how long you want to focus, and hit start. Every so often it checks in with Yes, No and Snooze buttons. Every time you say no or ignore it, it gets angrier, checks in more often, and gives you less time to answer. You can pick gentle, normal or drill mode depending on how much yelling you can handle. It also keeps track of your streaks, badges, ranks and mood, and it has a to-do list, a daily challenge, and a stats page that shows when you focus best and what distracts you the most.

Open the link, type a task, pick a preset like 25 minutes of work and a 5 minute break, and press Start focus block. When the check-in shows up at the bottom, answer with the buttons or the Y, N and S keys. Try clicking No a few times just to watch the page freak out. When the block ends you'll get a focus score and maybe a badge, and the Stats tab has everything else.

It's just one HTML file with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no frameworks, so you can also run it by web on your computer. Everything you do is saved locally in your own browser, so you don't need to make an account!

have fun locking in ;D
